## My Favorite Series: Wednesday

My favorite series right now is **Wednesday**. It is a great mix of mystery, comedy, and a little bit of horror. 

Here are my favorite things about the show:
* **Jenna Ortega** does an amazing job playing Wednesday Addams.
* The *Nevermore Academy* setting is really cool.
* I love Wednesday's pet scorpion, Nero, and her iconic braids.

If you haven't watched it yet, you can check out the trailer [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Di310WS8zLk).
